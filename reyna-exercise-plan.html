Why This Program Is Different From Walking

Your dedication to 1–2 hours of daily walking shows tremendous commitment — but walking alone at age 60 does not build the muscle mass needed to change your body composition, boost your metabolism, or support medication reduction. Cardio burns calories during the workout; muscle burns calories around the clock, including while you sleep.

This program deliberately reduces your daily cardio walking and replaces it with resistance training 3 days per week. This is not a step backward — it is the single most important change for your goals. Women at 60 lose muscle rapidly without deliberate resistance work, and thyroid conditions make this even more pronounced. The results you want require this shift.

🦋 Thyroid Considerations

  • Take thyroid medication first thing in the morning, 30–60 minutes before exercise and breakfast
  • Fatigue on some days is normal with thyroid conditions — listen to your body and reduce intensity, not frequency
  • Resistance training actively supports thyroid function by improving insulin sensitivity and metabolic rate
  • Track energy levels weekly — improving energy is a key sign your medication and exercise are working together
  • If fatigue is severe and persistent after 4 weeks, discuss medication timing adjustment with your doctor
  • Avoid high-intensity cardio on days when fatigue is significant — a gentle walk is always the right substitute

Wrist Guide Safe Exercises + Stretches

Wrist — What To Do vs. What To Avoid

Resistance bands are your primary tool because they allow a neutral wrist position that dumbbells and floor exercises do not
✅ Wrist-Safe Exercises
  • All resistance band exercises
  • Wall push-ups (hands flat on wall)
  • Bird dog on forearms (not hands)
  • Forearm plank (not on hands)
  • Any exercise where wrist stays neutral
  • Band exercises with loop around wrist, not grip
❌ Avoid — Wrist Stress
  • Floor push-ups
  • Downward dog (yoga)
  • Heavy dumbbell exercises
  • Plank on hands
  • Bird dog on hands if painful
  • Any grip that causes wrist pain
🔶 Wrist Warm-Up (Every Workout)
  • Wrist circles × 10 each direction
  • Gentle finger flexion and extension
  • Prayer stretch (palms together, lower hands)
  • Reverse prayer stretch (backs of hands together)
  • Wrist flexor stretch (arm extended, palm up, gently pull fingers back)

Knee — Safe Exercises + Modifications

Strengthening the muscles around the knee (glutes, quads, hamstrings) is the best long-term treatment for knee pain
✅ Knee-Safe Exercises
  • Glute bridges (no knee stress)
  • Clamshells (hip strengthening)
  • Side-lying leg lifts
  • Wall squats (partial range)
  • Reverse lunges (easier than forward)
  • Heel slides (gentle mobility)
  • Calf raises (hold wall)
  • Seated leg extensions (light band)
❌ Avoid — Knee Stress
  • Deep squats past 90°
  • Forward lunges
  • Step-ups higher than 4–6 inches
  • Running or jogging
  • Kneeling on hard floor without pad
  • Any exercise that causes knee pain during

Daily Wrist Stretch Routine (5 min)

1. Prayer Stretch

Press palms together in front of chest. Slowly lower hands toward waist while keeping palms together. Hold 30 sec. Stretches wrist flexors.

2. Reverse Prayer

Press backs of hands together, fingers pointing down. Hold 20–30 sec. Stretches the opposite side of the wrist.

3. Wrist Flexor Stretch

Extend one arm forward, palm up. Use other hand to gently pull fingers back toward you. Hold 30 sec each side.

4. Wrist Extensor Stretch

Extend one arm forward, palm down. Use other hand to gently press fingers downward. Hold 30 sec each side.

5. Wrist Circles

Make a loose fist. Slowly rotate wrists in full circles — 10 forward, 10 backward. Do this before AND after every workout.

Protein Is Non-Negotiable for Your Goals

Resistance training only builds muscle if there is enough protein available. At 60 with a thyroid condition, your target is 90–100g of protein daily. Most women eat far less than this. Without adequate protein, resistance training produces minimal results regardless of effort.

🥚 Breakfast Target

25–30g protein · 3 eggs + cottage cheese, or Greek yogurt + protein smoothie

🍗 Lunch Target

30–35g protein · Grilled chicken, salmon, or tuna · Generous portion

🐟 Dinner Target

30–35g protein · Fish or chicken primary · Eggs as backup option

📊 Track for 2 Weeks

Use a simple app like Cronometer to verify you're hitting 90–100g. Most women are surprised how far under they are.

8-Week Progression Plan

Weeks 1–2

Learn the movements with light or no resistance. Focus on wrist and knee positioning. Do not push through joint pain.

Weeks 3–4

Add light band resistance. Establish 3 resistance days as a non-negotiable habit. Note energy levels daily.

Weeks 5–6

Progress to medium resistance bands. Add a third round to the Friday circuit if energy allows.

Weeks 7–8

Assess progress. Energy should be noticeably improved. Discuss medication review timing with doctor if energy and markers are improving.

Month 3+

Consider adding light dumbbells for lower body exercises where wrists are not involved. Track body composition, not just weight.

⛔ Stop Exercising Immediately If You Experience

Sharp Wrist PainDull aching after exercise is normal initially. Sharp pain during a movement means stop that exercise immediately and rest the wrist.
Knee Pain During MovementDiscomfort is okay; pain is a stop signal. Reduce range of motion first, then weight, then stop the exercise if pain continues.
Extreme FatigueDifferent from normal workout tiredness. If you feel unwell, dizzy, or unusually exhausted, stop and rest. May indicate thyroid-related fatigue.
Heart Pounding or PalpitationsStop immediately, sit down, breathe slowly. With thyroid conditions, palpitations warrant medical attention if they occur during exercise.
Numbness or Tingling in HandsStop any exercise where this occurs. Can indicate nerve compression at the wrist that needs rest and possibly evaluation.
Knee Swelling After ExerciseSome soreness next day is normal. Visible swelling or warmth in the knee after exercise means rest that day and ice if needed.
