Gut Reset Protocol

Greg & Reyna's Low-Gas Meal Plan

2-Week Digestive Reset · Gluten-Free · Low-Carb · Anti-Inflammatory

Why This Plan Works

This two-week reset eliminates the most common gas-producing foods from your cookbook while keeping your meals satisfying, nutritious, and aligned with your weight loss and blood sugar goals. Every recipe is built around low-FODMAP vegetables — zucchini, spinach, green beans, romaine, cucumber, asparagus, and Swiss chard — which are widely available in Valle de Ángeles.

  • All onion replaced with garlic-infused olive oil and chives or green onion tops only
  • All garlic replaced with garlic-infused olive oil (the fructans stay in the bulb, the flavor comes through)
  • Cruciferous vegetables (broccoli, cauliflower, Brussels sprouts) temporarily removed
  • Beans and legumes temporarily removed — reintroduce in Week 3
  • Bell peppers temporarily removed — substitute zucchini or cucumber

Key Ingredient Swaps

Alliums
✕ Raw garlic & onion
✓ Garlic-infused olive oil + chives
Cruciferous
✕ Broccoli, cauliflower, cabbage
✓ Zucchini, green beans, Swiss chard
Legumes
✕ Black beans, chickpeas, lentils
✓ Extra eggs, chicken, or fish protein
Bell Peppers
✕ Red, green, yellow peppers
✓ Cucumber, zucchini, asparagus
Smoothie Sweeteners
✕ Sugar alcohols (erythritol, xylitol)
✓ Stevia or monk fruit only
Cooking Method
✕ Raw or lightly cooked veg
✓ Fully cook all vegetables until tender

Daily Digestive Support Tips

🍵 Ginger Tea Before Meals

Steep fresh ginger in hot water for 5 min before lunch and dinner. Well-evidenced for reducing bloating and improving gastric motility.

🌿 Fennel After Meals

Chew ½ tsp fennel seeds after dinner. A traditional remedy with solid research behind it for reducing gas production.

🥄 Apple Cider Vinegar

1 tsp in 4 oz water before meals. Supports stomach acid production and can reduce bloating, especially useful for Reyna.

💊 Magnesium Timing

Take your magnesium glycinate with dinner. It supports gut motility, which helps move gas through more efficiently.

🍽️ Slow Down

Aim for 20 minutes per meal minimum. Chew thoroughly — the digestive process begins in the mouth. Avoid eating while standing or distracted.

💧 Hydration Timing

Drink water between meals rather than with food. Large amounts of water during meals can dilute stomach acid and slow digestion.

Week 3+ Reintroduction Plan

After two weeks on this reset plan, your gut microbiome will have adapted and inflammation should be reduced. Reintroduce foods one at a time, one per week, and note any symptoms in the days following. This tells you which specific foods are problematic for each of you — your tolerances may differ.

Keep a simple note on your phone: date, food reintroduced, any symptoms within 48 hours.
